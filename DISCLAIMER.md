# Disclaimer and Statement of Purpose

## Why DaNTe exists

DaNTe is a general-purpose private communication tool. It is built on the premise
that private correspondence is a normal and legitimate need — recognised as a
human right in Article 12 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and
Article 8 of the European Convention on Human Rights — and that people are
entitled to communicate without their conversations being collected, profiled,
sold, or indefinitely retained.

Its explicit design goal is protection against **mass surveillance and bulk data
collection**, in particular by large commercial platforms and data brokers whose
business model is the accumulation of personal messages and metadata. Everyone
should be able to have a private chat.

## What the project is — and is not

- The DaNTe project publishes **source code**. It does **not** operate servers,
  relays, or bootstrap infrastructure as a central or commercial service, and has
  **no technical ability to read, decrypt, monitor, moderate, log, or remove**
  communications between users.
- **Servers and relay nodes are run by independent third parties.** Each operator
  alone is responsible for their own node, its configuration, and whatever
  content or conduct they choose to host or allow. Operators are not agents of,
  employed by, or controlled by the project or its contributors.
- Server operators have administrative tools (roles, bans, content removal) for
  the communities they host. Complaints about a specific server should be
  directed to that server's operators, who are the parties able to act.

## Lawful use only

The contributors to DaNTe **do not support, encourage, facilitate, or condone any
unlawful activity of any kind.** DaNTe is intended solely for lawful private
communication.

Users are solely responsible for their own conduct and for complying with every
law that applies to them, including laws governing the use, import, or export of
encryption software in their jurisdiction. If you cannot use this software
lawfully where you are, do not use it.

## Legal process

Because no project-operated system holds user messages, keys, or identities, the
project **cannot** produce message content or deanonymise users in response to a
legal demand — that data does not exist in its possession. Information published
to the DaNTe ledger (that an identity or server exists, plus coarse activity
timestamps) is public to everyone by design. Requests concerning a particular
community should go to that server's operators.
